# Decision memo

**Recommendation:** Seek approval for the mitigation plan only after confirming vendor savings, marketing feasibility, and currency exposure. Do not approve the original downside plan as meeting the 25% margin target.

|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Baseline | 2,400,000 | 1,730,000 | 670,000 | 27.92% | Yes |

| Mitigation at budget FX | 2,040,000 | 1,523,000 | 517,000 | 25.34% | Yes |
| Mitigation with FX stress | 2,040,000 | 1,636,000 | 404,000 | 19.80% | No |

These are calculated synthetic scenario outputs, not observed business outcomes.

## Decision drivers
The attendance reduction removes $360,000 in ticket revenue but saves only $132,000 in variable costs. Profit falls by $228,000.

The mitigation reduces fixed costs by $55,000 at the budget rate and marketing by $20,000, increasing profit by $75,000. Per-person cost and sponsorship fulfillment spending remain unchanged.

At $2,040,000 revenue, a 25% margin requires $510,000 profit. The mitigation has only **$7,000 headroom** at the peg. FX stress adds $113,000 in costs and leaves a **$106,000 shortfall** against the target.

Baseline break-even is 825 attendees; mitigation break-even is 693 at budget FX. Breaking even is not the same as achieving the target margin.

## Before approval
Confirm the vendor reductions in writing, determine whether marketing cuts threaten the attendance forecast, verify sponsor deliverables, and resolve the FX exposure. If the stress exposure cannot be addressed, return to leadership with a revised plan or an explicit margin exception.

This is a portfolio decision exercise, not financial advice or a recommendation for a real event.

# ATC transcription and research-based verification

**Evidence status:** Process narrative about prior transcription and review work. No proprietary audio or original submissions are included. This page does not provide a replayable accuracy test.

## My role
I transcribed technical aviation audio and later reviewed other submissions. Difficult passages required research into call signs, flight numbers, waypoints, arrival procedures, airport diagrams, and flight history alongside repeated listening.

My aviation-operations background helped me identify what needed verification. It did not make an unclear recording certain.

## Verification workflow
1. Listen and identify the unclear word, number, or speaker.
2. Record candidate interpretations without treating them as confirmed.
3. Check appropriate references for the recording's location and date.
4. Return to the audio and compare candidates with the sounds and surrounding transmissions.
5. Retain uncertainty when the evidence is insufficient; use the project's uncertainty notation or escalation process.

For a waypoint, I might check whether candidates exist on the relevant procedure. For a call sign, I might compare clearer transmissions from the same aircraft. A plausible route or historic flight match supports a hypothesis; it does not override contradictory audio.

## Reviewer judgment
I paid particular attention to call signs, runway numbers, and clearances because small transcription differences can change meaning. Corrections needed evidence from the recording, with references used for corroboration rather than substitution.

I would not correct a submission simply because another phrase sounded more familiar. Where the recording remained ambiguous, the review needed to preserve that ambiguity.

## What a reproducible sample would add
A permitted audio excerpt, recording date/context where available, timestamped transcript, uncertainty annotations, reference links, and an explanation for each correction. No word-error-rate or other accuracy figure is claimed without such a scored sample.

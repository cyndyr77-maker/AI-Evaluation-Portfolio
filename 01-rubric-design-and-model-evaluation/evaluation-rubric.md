# Evaluation rubric

Each item is binary: award all listed points only when the stated condition is met. Otherwise award zero. Maximum: 100. This deliberately small rubric favors scoring consistency over fine-grained stylistic judgments.

| Criterion | Points | Full-credit condition |
|---|---:|---|
| Choice | 20 | Selects B provisionally, subject to parking re-entry confirmation |
| Budget | 15 | Correctly states $92,000 is within the $95,000 event budget |
| Layout | 15 | Uses B's adjacent meeting rooms as a reason |
| Parking | 15 | Explicitly says re-entry remains unconfirmed |
| Evidence | 15 | Makes no unsupported factual claims about prices, facilities, or concessions |
| Requested structure | 10 | One choice, exactly two reasons, one tradeoff, exactly one confirmation |
| Tradeoff | 5 | States B costs $8,000 more than A |
| Clarity | 5 | The decision and contract condition are explicit and unambiguous |

Apply the lowest applicable cap after summing points:
- A recommendation exceeding the budget without a supported remedy: maximum 40.
- Claims that an unconfirmed mandatory requirement is confirmed: maximum 50.
- Material invented facts used to justify the decision: maximum 50.

90–100: strong demonstration; 75–89: acceptable with corrections; below 75: revise. These are portfolio thresholds, not an employer's scoring system.

Reviewers should cite the response text for every awarded criterion. The task explicitly defines the preferred provisional choice; in an open-ended real procurement task, other recommendations may be defensible.
